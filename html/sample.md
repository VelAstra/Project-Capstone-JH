# Welcome to AeroMark! ✨

AeroMark is a premium, beautifully crafted Markdown editor and reader. You are currently viewing the **Split Mode**, but you can toggle **Reader Mode** on the top right for a distraction-free experience.

## Features at a Glance
- **Real-time preview** with synchronized scrolling
- **GitHub Flavored Markdown** with tables and checklists
- **Themes**: Switch between Dark, Light, Sepia, and Aurora!
- **Interactive TOC**: Click the headers in the sidebar to jump.
